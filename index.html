Scenario: Should Telkara Broadband Buy Voice AI?

Telkara Broadband is an example client, with illustrative figures.

The Client: A home broadband provider where about 20,000 customer contracts end each month, and each customer who renews is worth about RM 750.

The Problem: 60 human agents, costing RM 4,500 a month each, spend their days on routine renewal calls, and many customers are not reached before their contract ends.

The Decision: Is voice AI worth RM 60,000 a month plus RM 150,000 to set up? AI would take the routine renewal calls, while complaints and cancellations stay with human agents.

A Real Reference: A published case study reports that PLDT, a telecom company, gained 33% productivity and halved call handling time with Talkbot Pro, in collections support (case study). None of the figures below are PLDT's.
