Signal Weights

What I Would Need

  • Past Churn: Which accounts left or shrank, and when.
  • Data Access: Call volumes, outcome tags, QM scores, the ticket system and the QBR log, per account.
  • A Baseline: What was promised to each client at the sale, in writing.
  • A Portfolio Figure: The size of each client's own base, so a volume drop can be read fairly.

How I Would Test It

  • Backtest: Score past quarters using only what was known then, and see how early it would have flagged the accounts that left.
  • Shadow Run: Run it beside the customer success team for one quarter, and compare its flags with their judgement.
  • Then Decide: Keep the weights, retune them, or drop the signals that add nothing.

What This Does Not Prove

  • Accuracy: No accuracy figure is shown, because on made-up data it would mean nothing.
  • Live Use: It has not run on a real customer base.
  • Your Current Process: You may already track this another way, and I do not know how.
  • Revenue Link: I do not know how contracts are billed, so a volume drop may or may not reduce revenue.

What I Would Ask You

  • How do you spot an at-risk account today, and how early?
  • Where do most accounts stall or shrink?
  • Is the QM data available per client, for clients who do not buy QM?
